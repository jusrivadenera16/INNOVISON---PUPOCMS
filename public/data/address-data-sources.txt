Address lookup data bundled for local use

Philippine administrative areas
- Dataset snapshot: PSGC Third Quarter 2026, dated 2026-10-12.
- Official source: https://psa.gov.ph/classification/psgc
- Normalized source snapshot: https://github.com/bendlikeabamboo/barangay-data-repository/tree/main/2026-10-12
- The bundled flat JSON preserves PSGC codes and parent codes. It contains 42,010 barangay records.

Postal codes
- The two postal JSON files are local copies of the datasets the Health Forms previously fetched at runtime:
  https://github.com/maikkoko/postal-ph/blob/master/data/postal_codes/_all.json
  https://github.com/maikkoko/postal-ph/blob/master/data/cities/cities_with_id.json
- These files are bundled to keep the existing ZIP autofill lookup available without a browser-to-GitHub request.
- ZIP lookup coverage should be checked against PHLPost's locator when refreshing the files:
  https://phlpost.gov.ph/zip-code-locator/
